Why AI Sim Lab

Intuitive Machine Learning Without Black Boxes

Most tools give you a static line instantly. AI Sim Lab opens up the hood so you can see weights iterate and error surfaces collapse.

Simple 4-Step Process

How You Learn With AI Sim Lab

STEP 01

Pick or Draw Data

Select built-in datasets (Ad Spend, Housing, Runge) or click the plot area to place custom data points.

STEP 02

Select Model & Degree

Choose Linear, Polynomial (Degree 1 through 6), or Exponential model families to evaluate capacity.

STEP 03

Watch GD Learn

Press Train and watch learning rate sliders control epoch-by-epoch convergence and loss reduction.

STEP 04

Inspect & Predict

Analyze $R^2$, Mean Squared Error, and residuals bar chart, then make test predictions instantly.
